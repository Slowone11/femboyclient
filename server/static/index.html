How to use it
  • Click the picture to click the game. Right click uses the held item. Shift+click works in chests and inventories. The wheel scrolls.
  • No menu open: a click goes where the crosshair points (the ring in the middle). Menu open: it lands exactly where you click.
  • Keys (with the picture selected): 1–9 pick a hotbar slot, Esc closes a menu. With a menu open, everything you type goes into it (search boxes, signs, anvils).
  • Input needs the game controls below unlocked (the admin token). Switch Input off here to stop this page acting in a game. In the game, /liveinput off and /liveview off do the same.
  • Only this PC can open this tab, and nothing is saved. A still screen costs almost no bandwidth.
Game controls

Player Finder

  1. Search a username.
  2. If they are online in SkyBlock, switch on Find player.
  3. Your game hops lobbies in their world until they show up in your tab list: /warp, /is, /warp…, one command every 5 seconds. It stops after 30 hops.

Your game types these commands itself. Automated commands can break Hypixel's rules, so use this at your own risk.

Turn the toggle off to search for someone else.
